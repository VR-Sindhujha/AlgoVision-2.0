🧠 Why Graph?

Graph represents the logistics network.

Warehouses, hubs and destinations become nodes.

Road connections become edges.

Used before running Dijkstra's Algorithm.

🚚 Graph Usage In AlgoVision

Customer places an order.

Graph creates route network.

Dijkstra finds shortest path.

Dynamic Programming optimizes ETA.

Final optimized route is delivered.

⚡ Complexity

Graph Traversal → O(V + E)

V = Nodes

E = Edges
